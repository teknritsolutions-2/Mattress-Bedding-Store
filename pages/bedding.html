What’s inside your pillow?

Solid memory foam
A single foam core can hold a flat or contoured profile.
Adjustable fill
A zip gives access to the shredded foam so you can add or remove fill to change loft.
Ventilated latex
Holes in the elastic foam provide paths for air through the core.
Cooling covers
A cool-touch textile can affect the first sensation at contact. Phase-change textiles temporarily absorb or release heat within their transition range.

A guide to fabric and fill

Compare the feel, weave and fit that suit your bed.

Cotton percale
Plain weave with a crisp hand and a matte surface.
Cotton sateen
Longer yarn floats give it a smoother feel and more sheen.
Washed linen
Flax fibre with a textured hand and naturally irregular surface.
Polyester fill
Fill weight and quilting influence loft and how evenly the fill stays in place.
Quilted protectors
Stitched padding adds a layer over the mattress. Match the skirt depth to the mattress height.

Complete your bed

Pair a smooth fitted sheet with a pillow at your preferred height and a quilted cover with enough overhang.

Care for each layer

Pillow
Wash the cover as labelled. Spot-clean a foam core only if its care guidance allows it.
Sheets
Sort by colour and follow the fabric’s wash and drying instructions.
Duvet
Air regularly. Check the label and the care instructions for its fill before washing.
Protector
Follow the specified drying temperature, particularly for protectors with a waterproof backing.

Find the mattress underneath

Match your protector pocket and fitted sheet to both the mattress dimensions and the height.
