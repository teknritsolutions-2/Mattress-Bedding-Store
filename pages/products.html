Understand mattress layers

The order and number of layers vary by model.

  1. CoverThe outer fabric sits over the upholstery. Its fibre and weave affect how it feels to the touch.
  2. Comfort layerFoam, fibre or latex shapes the first sensation when you lie down.
  3. Transition layerAn intermediate material can bridge the soft top and the denser support core.
  4. Support coreSprings or dense foam form the base of the mattress.
