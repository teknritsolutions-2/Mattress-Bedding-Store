Prepare for your visit

What should I bring?

Note your frame’s inside length, width and base height. Bring the names of any products you want to compare.

What can I compare?

Ask about mattress firmness, pillow loft, sheet depth and care instructions for each material.

Is a time confirmed?

A preferred date and time help describe your request. The store must confirm availability separately.

Visit and product FAQs

How do I choose a size?

Measure the width and length inside your frame. Compare these figures with the dimensions under each product’s size options.

How do I compare mattress types?

Use the mattress comparison for construction, thickness and firmness. Keep the size the same when comparing indicative prices.

Are the prices final?

Prices are indicative. Ask the store to confirm specifications, availability and your final quote.

How can I check availability?

Include the model and size you want to see in your enquiry. Ask the store to confirm availability.

How do I prepare a visit request?

Choose your interest and preferred time, review the details, then copy or download the request to share with the store.

Where do I confirm policies?

Check the Trial & Returns page for the questions to ask. Confirm the terms that apply to your purchase through the store’s confirmed contact channel.
