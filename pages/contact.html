Before your visit

What should I bring?

Note your frame’s inside length, width and base height. Bring the names of any products you want to compare.

What can I compare?

Ask about mattress firmness, pillow loft, sheet depth and care instructions for each material.

Is a time confirmed?

A preferred date and time help describe your request. The store must confirm availability separately.
