Compare mattress constructions

Read the construction alongside the firmness rating. Two models rated medium can feel different.

Pocket springs
Individually sleeved coils compress on their own. A connected spring unit responds across the surface.
Memory foam
Slow recovery after pressure gives the foam its contouring feel.
Latex & hybrids
Springy latex can sit over a spring core as the comfort layer in a hybrid.
Zoned support
Coil tension or foam firmness changes across the mattress core.
