Start with fit and feel

Use three simple checks to narrow your mattress shortlist.

SizeMeasure the inside of your frame and check the model’s exact length and width.
ConstructionCompare connected springs, pocket springs and layered foams.
FirmnessUse plush, medium or firm as a starting point, then compare models.

Look closely at the materials

Cover fabric, seam finish and quilting shape the surface you see and touch. Compare the materials listed for each item and how they need to be cared for.
