About Fold & Field

Comfort is a personal thing.

One person loves a slow-settling mattress. Another prefers the lift of springs. Our collection is organised around those differences, so you can start with what feels right to you.

Explore the collection
Our approach

Choose by touch, fit and finish.

Touch

Look beneath the cover. Spring, memory foam and firmer layered cores each respond differently to weight and movement.

Fit

Check the dimensions of each model against your frame, then consider the combined height of base and mattress.

Finish

Pair the surface with sheets, protectors and pillows that suit your laundry routine and the temperature of your room.
