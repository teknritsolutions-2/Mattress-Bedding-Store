Explore mattresses and bedding by the specifications that affect everyday use, from mattress height to pillow loft and sheet depth.

Explore our approach

Start with fit and feel

Size
Measure the inside of the frame and check dimensions on the model page. A size name by itself may not tell you the length or width.
Construction
Compare connected springs, individually wrapped coils and layered foams. The core and comfort layers contribute differently to the feel.
Firmness
Use plush, medium and firm as starting points, then compare the comfort layers and base for the feel you prefer.

Look closely at the materials

Cover fabric, seam finish and quilting shape the surface you see and touch. Compare the materials listed for each item and how they need to be cared for.
