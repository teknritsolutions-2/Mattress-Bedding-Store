Prepare a return enquiry

  1. Find the purchase details

    Locate your order reference, purchase date, product and size.

  2. Confirm the terms

    Ask which trial or return terms apply to your purchase and its condition.

  3. Contact the store

    Use the business’s confirmed contact channel and describe the request.

  4. Follow the instructions

    Keep the item until the store clarifies the next steps and any transport arrangements.

Questions by product

Mattresses
Ask whether your category, size and purchase channel qualify for a trial. Confirm base, condition and packaging requirements.
Pillows and protectors
Ask whether opening the product or using it affects eligibility and what hygiene conditions apply.
Sheets and duvets
Check applicable time limits and whether labels or packaging need to be intact.
Multiple-item purchases
Buying a mattress together with pillows or bedding? Confirm whether trial, return and hygiene conditions apply separately to each product.
