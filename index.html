Inside the mattress

What changes the feel?

Spring

Coils give the surface a responsive bounce. The upholstery above them determines how softly you settle in.

Memory foam

Foam gradually follows your shape. Compare the depth of the comfort layer if you prefer a closer feel.

Orthopaedic

Here this describes a firmer, layered support construction. Try the surface to judge your own comfort.

Bedroom comfort

Layer from the sheets up.

Start with a fitted sheet that suits your mattress depth. Add a pillow with a comfortable loft, then choose a duvet that feels right for the room.

Sleep notes

Small habits for the bedroom.

Keep layers flexible

Keep a light cover within reach so you can adjust as the room cools.

Give bedding some air

Pull the covers back while you get ready. Follow the care label when it is time to wash.

Ease into evening

Choose a regular wind-down time and soften the lighting before bed.
