Discover mattress construction

Pocket springs
Fabric sleeves wrap individual coils so each can compress independently. Bonnell coils connect across the unit and respond as an assembly.
Memory foam
Viscoelastic foam gradually conforms under pressure and recovers after you move. An open-cell formulation changes the structure of the foam.
Latex & hybrids
Latex compresses and rebounds differently from memory foam. A hybrid combines comfort layers with a spring support core.
Zoned support
Some cores vary coil tension or foam firmness by area. Compare the position of the zones with your usual sleeping position.

Sleep & care

Compare day-to-day care when choosing each layer.

Mattresses
Check the base, rotation and cleaning instructions for your exact model. Rotate head to foot if its care guide recommends it.
Sheets
Follow the label’s wash temperature and dry thoroughly before putting sheets back on the bed.
Pillow loft
Compare how high your head sits once the fill compresses. A loft that suits one sleeper may feel too high or low to another.
Bedroom comfort
Keep bedding layers easy to add and remove as the room changes through the evening.
