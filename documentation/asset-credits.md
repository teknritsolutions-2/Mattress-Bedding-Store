Each photo links to its original source and license. The commercial-use terms are listed per photo. All photos were resized as needed and encoded as WebP. The card variants use a 3:2 crop. The latex pillow card adds a matching white background margin to preserve its full silhouette. Documented dimensions are the delivered WebP dimensions.
